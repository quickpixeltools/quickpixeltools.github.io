What Is an Image Compressor?

An image compressor reduces the file size of an image. Smaller image files can be easier to store, share and use on websites.

How to Compress an Image

Choose your JPG, PNG or WebP image, select the compression quality and click the Compress Image button. After processing, you can download the compressed image.

Is It Free?

Yes. QuickPixel Tools provides this image compressor as a free online tool.

Are My Images Uploaded?

The image processing on this page is performed directly inside your browser. Your selected image does not need to be uploaded to a QuickPixel Tools server for compression.

Why Compress Images?

Large image files can take more storage space and may take longer to upload or share. Compressing images can reduce their file size while maintaining an appropriate level of visual quality.
