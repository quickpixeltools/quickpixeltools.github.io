1. Introduction

Welcome to QuickPixel Tools. We respect your privacy and aim to explain clearly how information may be handled when you visit or use this website.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be provided directly to QuickPixel Tools, how our browser- based image tools handle selected files, and how cookies, advertising services and other third-party services may operate on the website.

2. Images Processed by Our Tools

The QuickPixel Image Compressor, Image Resizer and JPG to PNG Converter are designed to process selected images directly inside your web browser.

For the basic processing provided by these tools, selected images do not need to be uploaded to a QuickPixel Tools server. The processing is performed by your browser using your device's available resources.

Because processing takes place on your device, performance may depend on your browser, device capabilities, available memory and the size and dimensions of the selected image.

QuickPixel Tools does not claim that every future feature or third-party service will operate entirely in the browser. If the technical design of a tool changes, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.
