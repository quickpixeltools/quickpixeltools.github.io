What Is an Image Resizer?

An image resizer changes the width and height of an image to specific dimensions. This can be useful when an image needs to fit a particular website, application or project.

How to Resize an Image

Choose your image, enter the desired width and height in pixels, then click the Resize Image button. When processing is complete, you can download your resized image.

Why Resize Images?

Different websites and applications may require images with specific dimensions. Resizing an image can help it fit those requirements.
