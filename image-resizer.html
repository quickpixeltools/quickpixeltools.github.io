What Is an Image Resizer?

An image resizer changes the dimensions of an image, such as its width and height. Resizing can help you prepare images for websites, social media, documents and other digital uses.

How to Resize an Image

Choose your JPG, PNG or WebP image, enter the desired width and height in pixels, and click the Resize Image button. You can then download your resized image.

Why Resize Images?

Different websites and applications may require images with specific dimensions. Resizing an image can help you meet those requirements and make images easier to use.
